## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for industrial logistics, rugged mobile handhelds (PDAs), mounted forklift terminals, and rapid barcode/document scanning workflows. The operational environment is harsh, variable in lighting, fast-paced, and physically demanding. 

The aesthetic blends **Tactile Industrial Utility** with **High-Contrast Digital HUD** paradigms. It prioritizes zero-latency recognition, massive touch-hit clearance for gloved operators, and distinct visual statuses that cut through warehouse glare. Feedback is instantaneous, explicit, and definitive, eliminating ambiguity in repetitive intake cycles.

## Colors

The system uses a focused dark operational palette optimized for low battery consumption on OLED mobile scanners and minimal operator eye strain in dim inventory bays:

- **Primary (`#00E082`)**: Tactical Emerald Green. Serves as the primary operational confirmation color (active scanner state, successful scan registration, verified status, active session highlights).
- **Secondary (`#FF334B`)**: Laser Red. Reserved strictly for duplicates, mis-scans, unreadable barcodes, and blocking critical alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB300`)**: Warning Amber. Denotes pending verification, routing exceptions, or manual intervention flags.
- **Neutral (`#111418`)**: Deep Industrial Carbon. Forms the solid non-distracting bedrock, paired with `#1A1F26` for panel elevations and `#FFFFFF` / `#E6EDF5` for primary data readouts.

## Typography

The type hierarchy is split into two specialized roles:
1. **Interface Structure & Narrative (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Clean, solid, geometric grotesk for screen labels, instructions, navigation, and modal dialogues. Offers immediate scan-legibility without decorative flourishes.
2. **Operational Data & Tracking (`JetBrains Mono`)**: High-visibility monospace for serial numbers, BOL (Bill of Lading) codes, pallet IDs, and raw hardware scan feeds. Explicit character distinctions (zero with dot/slash, distinct `1`, `l`, and `I`) safeguard against identification errors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive fluid-grid framework calibrated for rugged device constraints (Zebra, Honeywell, and vehicle-mounted rugged tablets):

- **Mobile Rugged Handhelds (<600px)**: Single-column vertical stream. The top 25% of the viewport is reserved for current scanner status and HUD feedback; the bottom 30% houses oversized tactical action zones within thumb reach. 
- **Fixed/Vehicle Terminals (600px–1200px)**: 2-column split viewport. Left pane maintains the high-speed live intake queue; right pane displays the active document inspection, OCR preview, and validation controls.
- **Minimum Tap Targets**: 52px on mobile PDAs and 60px on mounted touchscreens to account for gloved operations and vibration.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is communicated primarily through **Tonal Surface Stacking** coupled with **High-Contrast Tactical Outlines**. Soft ambient blur-heavy drop shadows are avoided as they wash out under warehouse high-bay sodium and LED lighting.

- **Base Layer (Level 0)**: `#111418` canvas.
- **Card & Cell Containers (Level 1)**: `#1A2027` with a crisp 1px solid border in `#2A3441`.
- **Active Scanning Target / Focused Item (Level 2)**: Elevated with an inset 2px border in `#00E082` and an optional sharp 4px directional offset drop (`0px 4px 0px #000000`).
- **Tactile Depress States**: Active interactive components physically translate down 2px along the Y-axis to simulate physical mechanical switches upon contact.

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, structural curvature (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius). Sharp enough to maintain an industrial hardware feel, but softened slightly at 4px (`rounded-base`) and 8px (`rounded-lg`) to prevent optical pixel fraying on rugged low-DPI displays. Fully circular pills are avoided to maintain maximum square surface contact for touch inputs.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Switches
- **Primary Operational Action**: Background `#00E082`, text `#0A0D10` (weight 700), minimum height 52px. Features a bottom 3px offset border (`#00A85F`) creating a physical keycap aesthetic that collapses on press (`:active { transform: translateY(2px); }`).
- **Critical / Reject Button**: Background `#FF334B`, text `#FFFFFF`, 3px mechanical edge (`#B31225`).
- **Secondary Utility Action**: Dark carbon fill (`#222933`) with a 1.5px high-visibility border (`#3E4A5B`).

### Barcode & Ticket Identifiers
- Packaged inside monospace high-contrast badge blocks (`#0C0E11` background, `#00E082` or `#E6EDF5` text, bordered with 1px `#2A3441`).
- Letter spacing expanded to `0.06em` to prevent misreading contiguous characters under motion.

### Real-Time Intake Feed List
- Striped continuous list items with a 4px left-edge indicator bar:
  - Green (`#00E082`) for Verified/Stored.
  - Amber (`#FFB300`) for Pending Sync/OCR Review.
  - Red flashing or solid (`#FF334B`) for Duplicate/Mismatched SKU.
- Swipe gestures are disabled by default to prevent accidental dismissals during physical handling; actions require explicit tap confirmation.

### Data Inputs & Manual Override Fields
- Inactive: Inset background `#0C0E11`, border 1.5px `#2E3846`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Focused/Scanning: Border 2px `#00E082`, subtle high-contrast corner tick marks.
- Error/Duplicate: Border 2px `#FF334B` with persistent error readout anchored directly below the field in JetBrains Mono.

### Document/Scanner HUD Overlay
- Semi-transparent target reticle with four tactile corner brackets framing the active optical viewport.
- Dynamic color-shift of the reticle brackets: White (Idle/Searching), Emerald Green (Locked/Valid), Laser Red (Parse Error/Duplicate).
